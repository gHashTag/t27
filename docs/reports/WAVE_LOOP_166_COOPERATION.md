## Variant A — Formal Math Bridge with Baez & Schwahn

**Premise:** Their theorem rigorously embeds SM gauge group in F4 stabilizers of exceptional Jordan algebra. This is genuine publishable mathematics.
**Proposal:** Invite Baez & Schwahn to co-author a paper extending their stabilizer theorem to Trinity’s 600-cell spectral-action framework. Trinity provides Coq proof infrastructure + mass-prediction data; Baez & Schwahn provide Jordan-algebra rigor. Target: arXiv preprint within 90 days.
**Benefit:** Trinity gains peer-reviewed mathematical legitimacy. Baez & Schwahn gain a predictive physics application of their theorem.
**Risk:** Academic timelines are slow. Scope must be tightly bounded (single module: H4GaugeEmbedding ↔ Jordan stabilizers).

## Variant B — OPH Differentiation Brief + Open Challenge

**Premise:** OPH claims GR + SM from 5 axioms but has no hardware, no formal proofs, no measured predictions.
**Proposal:** Publish a public differentiation brief comparing Trinity vs OPH on falsifiability criteria: (1) hardware-software co-design, (2) Coq zero-Admitted policy, (3) seal-hash transparency, (4) neutrino mass bounds vs cosmology. Invite OPH authors to respond with equivalent deliverables.
**Benefit:** Positions Trinity as the only falsifiable, formally verified, hardware-backed geometric unification framework.
**Risk:** Public challenge may escalate into unproductive debate. Keep tone academic and invite constructive collaboration rather than confrontation.

## Variant C — Lean 4 ↔ Coq Conversion for GIFT Axiom Audit

**Premise:** GIFT now has 15 axioms (up from 4). Their growing axiom count is a relative weakness.
**Proposal:** Offer Trinity’s Coq infrastructure to independently audit GIFT’s 15 axioms and determine which are eliminable. Publish joint audit results. Trinity absorbs any eliminable axioms into its own Coq stack; GIFT benefits from external review.
**Benefit:** Demonstrates Trinity’s openness to rigorous external audit. May lead to collaboration on formal-methods standards for physics.
**Risk:** GIFT may decline external audit. Audit effort is non-trivial (1-2 engineer-weeks). Fallback: publish unilateral audit.
