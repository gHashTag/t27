## Q2 — does the `M²` term survive past `M = 25`?

Adder arms, first differences in LUT per 4 mantissa bits:

| E_t | 17→21 | 21→25 | 25→29 | 29→33 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 628 | 624 | 800 | **244** |
| 3 | 672 | 632 | 1128 | **540** |
| 4 | 796 | 880 | 788 | *(over budget)* |
| 5 | 960 | 680 | 1168 | *(over budget)* |
| 6 | 1116 | 812 | 1304 | *(over budget)* |

A surviving quadratic makes first differences **grow**. On both rows that reach
`M = 33` they **fall by a factor of three**. Refits:

| data | m2 | m1 | E_t | c | R² | n |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| published (post-route, M≤25) | 2.194 | 53.84 | −197.1 | 363.5 | 0.9989 | — |
| local yosys, M ≤ 25 | 0.849 | 172.08 | +330.0 | −891.6 | 0.9679 | 45 |
| local yosys, all M | **−0.057** | 195.66 | +393.2 | −1242.5 | 0.9628 | 52 |

Dropping the quadratic term entirely:

| subset | quadratic R² | linear R² |
|---|---:|---:|
| M ≤ 25 | 0.9679 | **0.9670** |
| M ≥ 25 | 0.9558 | 0.9335 |

**Answer: it does not survive.** Below `M = 25` the quadratic term buys
**0.0009 of R²** over a purely linear model — it is not supported by the data
even where the published fit lives. Including `M = 29, 33` drives the
coefficient **negative**. Growth flattens.

> **The published quadratic must be labelled a local fit**, which is exactly the
> contingency the docstring names.

## An unplanned third finding — the sign of the `E_t` coefficient

Published: **−197.1**. Local: **+330.0** (M ≤ 25) and **+393.2** (all M). A
negative coefficient says more exponent cells *reduce* LUT, which no datapath
argument supports.

The obvious suspect is collinearity, and it is **ruled out on this grid**:

```
corr(M, E_t)  = -0.0813      near-orthogonal by design
corr(E_t, b)  = +0.9934      collinear BY CONSTRUCTION, b = ceil(E_t log2 3)
condition number = 1696.4
LUT ~ M + E_t   coef = +393.5   R2 = 0.9628
LUT ~ M + b     coef = +259.2   R2 = 0.9616
```

`E_t` and its binary width `b` are interchangeable regressors (R² differs by
0.0012), so the model cannot separate them — but **both are positive**. The
disagreement with the published sign is therefore not a fitting artefact on this
side, and it needs the post-route arm to resolve.

**Do not change the article's coefficient on the strength of this record.** It
is pre-route on a different yosys. What it establishes is that the post-route
sweep must now be run, and that its result is not safely predictable from the
existing fit.
