## 1. Variant C rationale

The physical bench was unavailable for Wave 414:

- P12 (CFGCLK / CCLK_0) is not wired to a logic-analyzer channel.

1. Temperature/voltage/process-corner PVT envelope in Lean 4.
2. Extended VCD parser for multi-bit buses and real-valued analog nets.
3. `--validate` early-rejection for `tri fpga measured-to-lean --raw-ns`.
