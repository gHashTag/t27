overlap integral on the 600-cell:

m_l^(gen) = v · y_l · φ^(2(gen−3))

where gen = 1,2,3 for e, μ, τ respectively, v ≈ 246.22 GeV is the Higgs VEV,
and y_l are generation Yukawa couplings fixed by the Higgs-potential minimum.
