## A table for the sixth instance (Closes #3499)

- Five defects this campaign shared one shape: **a rule present in three backends and missing from the fourth** -- `Type.member`, `.` versus `->`, the brace literal, `s.len`, `cast_iN`. The cause is structural: four independent AST-to-text emitters, so a lowering written in one CANNOT reach the others, and the class recurs by construction. Each was found by accident and cost a full pass.
- `tools/backend_parity_table.py` already asks this about DECLARATION FORMS. `tools/builtin_parity_table.py` asks it about CALLS, which is the other axis, and **printed the sixth instance on its first run**: `abs` **392 uses in 45 specs**, `round` 136/38, `sqrt` 100/25, `floor` 98/15, `max` 51/26, `min` 19/12 -- all lowered by Rust and Zig, all passed through by C. **796 uses.** And `sign` 63/48 is the other kind of row: nobody lowers it, which is a question for the language, not a backend.
- For three of the six the spelling was **never wrong**: `sqrt(x)` IS C, and the only thing missing was `<math.h>`. The whole repair is a conditional include, decided the way `<string.h>` and `<assert.h>` already are. Errors **10 831 -> 10 811**, **10 files better and none worse**.
- **`abs` is deliberately excluded**, and the reason is the shape this campaign has refused four times: C has `abs` for `int` and `fabs` for `double`, and choosing without the argument's type is a silent truncation. `min` and `max` are not C functions at all. Those rows stay loud.
- **It cannot be a gate.** A blank column is not a defect by itself: `print` has no business in synthesizable Verilog and the float cast is refused deliberately in three backends. The table proposes; a person concludes -- three of the five earlier findings needed a real compiler to settle.
- **And the reader was wrong once, in exactly the way its sibling's docstring records.** The first version read Zig's `@abs(v)` as passthrough, because `abs(` occurs inside it, and called six lowered rows missing: a failure of the instrument printed as a fact about the backend. The `@` is in the lookbehind now and the self-check asserts that case.
