Cause, confirmed by 5 lab probes: `@intFromFloat` of a NaN gives 0 in t27b, by design, while the x86_64 reference build gives a target-dependent index (Zig calls this illegal behaviour). The real port keeps its guard, so libm.t27 is unaffected. Filed as #7445.
