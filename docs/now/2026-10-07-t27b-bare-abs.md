# NOW -- t27b: `abs(x)` with no `fn abs` is Zig's `@abs` (2026-10-07)

## A bare `abs` call on a float or a comptime operand (Closes #7227)

- New conformance spec `specs/tri/t27b/conformance/bare_abs.t27`. It gives 6 pass with 0 vacuous under `t27c test-report`, and the same 6 under t27b, with 20 runtime asserts.
- When a spec calls `abs(x)` and declares no `fn abs`, the reference emits Zig's `@abs(x)`. t27b now does the same for an `f32` or `f64` operand: it evaluates the operand once and selects `0 - x` when `x <= 0`, else `x`. That keeps `abs(-0.0)` at `+0.0`, sends infinities to `+inf`, and keeps NaN a NaN.
- A comptime float or integer operand is folded. A declared `fn abs` still wins.
- `abs` of a runtime integer is refused as `ExprCall(abs of an integer)`, because Zig's `@abs` of an `iN` is a `uN`. `abs` with two arguments and other bare builtins (`sqrt`, `min`, ...) stay `ExprCall(undeclared fn)`. These are refusals, not mismatches.
- 8 mutants each fail the same tests in t27b and in the reference. Of 12 probes, none disagree: 6 pass in both, 1 fails in both, 2 are blocked by the reference and refused by t27b, and 3 pass the reference and are refused by t27b (`abs` of `i32` and `u8`, and a module `const` of `-2.0`).
- Code is in `cli/t27b/src/lower.rs` and `cli/t27b/src/lower_float.rs`. Tests are in `cli/t27b/tests/source.rs`. All three files are listed in `tools/policy/foreign-exceptions.txt` under the owner's approval on #6063.
- Ledger `docs/reports/t27b_expectations.json`: `specs/rings/ring_103_phi_sgd.t27` (6 pass, 6 asserts) and the new spec move to pass.
Refs #6063. Closes #7227.
