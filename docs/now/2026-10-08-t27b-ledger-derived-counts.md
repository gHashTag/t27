# NOW -- the t27b ledger stores no total over its entries

## counts are derived from entries, as steward.t27 decides (Closes #7859)

- `steward.t27` gains `header_stored(field)`: a ledger header field is stored only when it is not a
  total over the entries. `counts` is one, `max_not_pass` is not (it is the ratchet's promise and
  a PR that adds a pass entry does not move it). 4 new tests and an invariant; the spec passes
  128 of 128 tests. `gen/c/tri/t27b/steward.c` is regenerated by `t27c gen-c`.
- `dump_ledger` writes only the fields the spec stores; `ledger_counts` derives the totals where
  they are printed. The committed ledger loses its `counts` block, nothing else.
- Why: on 2026-10-08 #7579, #7586 and #7626 went dirty three times in two hours on the `"pass": N`
  line alone, while their entries merged cleanly; the fpga jobs take an hour, so a spec PR could
  not outrun the t27b PRs landing on master.
- Test: two branches that bless one and two new pass specs merge without a conflict; the same
  merge with rules that store the counts conflicts (negative control). The ratchet's red cases and
  the UNEXPECTED PASS mutation control are unchanged and still red where they should be.
