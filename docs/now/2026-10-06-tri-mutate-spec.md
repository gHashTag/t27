# NOW -- tri mutate spec: the mutants a spec's own tests do not notice (2026-10-06)

## `tri mutate spec` (Closes #6993, slice of #6971)

- `tri mutate run` perturbs the numeric literals of a checker. `tri mutate spec --file F [--fn NAME]` mutates the functions of a `.t27` spec instead: it drops a guard (`if (c) { return x; }`), flips a comparison (`<` and `<=`, `>` and `>=`, `==` and `!=`), swaps `and` with `or` (and `&&` with `||`), and drops a `+ 1` or `- 1`. Bodies of `test` and `invariant` blocks are left alone.
- Each mutant is compiled with `t27c gen` and run with `zig test` in its own temp dir, `--jobs` at a time. A mutant is killed by a failing `zig test`, by a gen failure, or by a hang past `--timeout`. The rest are survivors, printed with line, kind and the mutated text.
- A survivor is a question, not a verdict: it is a test gap or an equivalent mutant, and only a reader can tell which. So the command exits 0 when every mutant ran, survivors or not. It exits 1 when a mutant could not be run at all; those are listed as NOT RUN with the cause and are counted as neither killed nor survived.
- Controls, on the lab (Railway, 48 cores): a copy of the actor spec with a guard no test reaches is reported as a drop-guard survivor on that line, and a copy with a boundary no test reaches as a flip-cmp survivor on that line. A planted gap that another test still covered was killed, and was right to be: a planted mutant is first applied by hand and must pass `zig test` before a kill counts as a tool bug.
- First full runs: `specs/queen/actors.t27` as merged in #6966, 105 of 110 killed (103 by `zig test`, 2 by a hang); the follow-up on #6971, 149 of 157 killed (146 and 3). Of the 8 survivors, 2 were test gaps (slot `GEN_MASK` and tag `TAG_MASK` are valid and no test said so) and 6 are equivalent (the commit on #6971 that closes the gaps names all 6 and why). The 62-pair hand list #6966 was checked with had tried none of #6966's 5.
- Two bugs of the first version, found on its own runs. A hang killed `zig test` but not the test binary it had started, which kept spinning at 100% CPU with PID 1 as parent; a timeout now stops and kills the children first, and a unit test starts a `sleep` grandchild and fails if it outlives the timeout. With sixteen parallel jobs one spawn failed, and the first version aborted all 110 mutants and dropped the cause, so it is not known (the lab caps a container at 1000 processes, the likeliest one). A spawn now retries on `EAGAIN`, a mutant that still cannot start is NOT RUN with its cause, and the lab runs `--jobs 8`.
- `cli/tri/src/mutate.rs` is Rust, a foreign language under `specs/policy/own_language.t27`. It is listed in `tools/policy/foreign-exceptions.txt` with the `owner-approved-foreign` label on #6993; the port to `.t27` is part of the self-host debt in #5980.
