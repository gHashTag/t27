# NOW -- refuse the task the Queen could never take (2026-09-24)

## The swarm was filling its own backlog with rows it cannot dispatch (Closes #4723)

- An issue with no `## Boundary` is not work the Queen declines, it is work she **cannot see**: she reserves files before dispatching, so with nothing to reserve the issue is skipped every round, for good. On 2026-09-23 that was 565 of 649 candidates, twenty lanes idle, `refusal="nothing to choose"` over six hundred open issues.
- Every one of them was opened by something that never checked. `feed_untested`, `feed_defects`, `feed_empty_bodies` and `refile` all called `gh issue create` with no gate. `needs_boundary.py` labels them a day later and `propose_boundary.py` drafts what is missing -- both are repairs after the fact.
- `tools/queen/task_shape.py` is the gate, and its rule is a **pinned twin** of `boundaryPathsOf` in `queen-tick.ts`. That matters in both directions: a gate stricter than the Queen rejects work she would have taken, and a looser one passes work she will silently skip -- which is the failure this exists to end. Verified not only against its own cases but against **40 issues she had already parsed: 40 agreements, 0 disagreements.**
- Pinned in `--self-test` because each surprised once: the section ends at the next `## ` heading and NOT at a blank line, a line contributes its FIRST path token and not every one, backticks and trailing punctuation are stripped, `### Boundary` is not the heading she reads, and `## Границы` is.
- It refuses, and it never closes: an issue may be a real problem badly filed, and deleting a person's report to keep a queue tidy is the wrong trade. `roadmap` and `epic` stay exempt, because a goal is not a task. A documentation-only boundary warns rather than refuses -- whether prose-only work may be dispatched is the operator's call, not this gate's.
- Alongside it, `propose_boundary.py --resolve-bare` reads a file named without its directory (`compiler.rs`, `octree.t27`) as the path the tree holds for it, when the tree holds exactly one -- 8025 of 8323 basenames are unique here. That found 65 more issues whose boundary was written all along, just without a directory in front of it. 83 more bodies written.
- The workflow that would apply the same gate to issues opened BY HAND is not in this change: pushing `.github/workflows/` needs a token carrying the `workflow` scope, and the one this ran under does not. The gate that matters more is here -- the feeds are where the 565 came from.
