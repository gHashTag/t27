## The count was right for the wrong reason (Refs #2161)

- Refs #2161. `tri gates mutate --boundary` reported 31 sites for tools/gft_backprop_microcode.py, and an independent tokeniser counted 31 comparisons before the `if __name__` block. Two numbers agreed and the story wrote itself. Both numbers were real; THE AGREEMENT WAS A COINCIDENCE
- Asking the tool for the site LINES instead of the count showed the scan ending at 371 -- nine lines before `__main__`, at `def self_check():`. `is_control_fn` sets `in_control` and `boundary_sites` never resets it, so everything from line 380 to end of file had silently never been mutated. 31 -> 62 sites after the fix, which is the tokeniser count for the whole file
- When two measurements agree, check they agree about a shared OBJECT, not a shared integer. The tokeniser answered "how many comparisons precede __main__"; the tool answered "how many sites did I find before I stopped". Nothing connected them but a number. Prefer the answer with more structure: a list over a count, a range over a total, a witness over a verdict
