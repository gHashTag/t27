# NOW -- The watchman sees a half-empty swarm, and refills it (2026-09-20)

## Four rules for partial idleness, and the first action the pusher takes (Closes #4308)

- Every rule in the pusher began `workers_active == 0`. So two bees in ten lanes read as healthy: measured at 10:16Z, right after a redeploy, `capacity 10, active 2, idle 8, utilization 20` with 221 uncovered specs waiting, and nothing fired.
- **`lanes-idle`** fires on two or more empty lanes while work is dispatchable. **`fuel-runway`** fires while the swarm is still running, when fewer issues are dispatchable than there are lanes -- a feeder takes minutes to build a compiler, so a rule that waits for the tank to empty is a rule that reports the outage. **`capacity-shrank`** fires when the lane count falls between readings: the cap lives in `TRIOS_QUEEN_MAX_WORKERS`, so it went from 16 to 8 with no commit anywhere to show for it. **`throughput-down`** fires when bees-finished-per-hour is half of what it was, which is the first time this system can answer "productivity fell" with a number.
- The rate is taken from the server's own `queue.observedAt`, not the runner's clock: a scheduled job that starts late would otherwise read as a swarm that slowed. A finished counter that went BACKWARDS is a restart, not a rate, and returns no reading at all.
- **The pusher now acts.** `out-of-fuel`, `fuel-runway` and `lanes-idle` all mean lanes with nothing to put in them, and the answer to all three is a feeder run. It dispatches `queen-feed-untested.yml` and `queen-feed-empty-bodies.yml`, at most one round per reading and never twice inside twenty minutes, and prints what it did into the pulse issue. It carries `actions: write` for exactly that. Nothing else can be dispatched: a watchdog that can run anything is a second Queen with none of her gates.
- **Its own false alarm, fixed.** `not-evolving` fired at 10:28Z with nine bees running and the total unchanged for sixteen minutes -- which is what a nine-bee swarm on a twenty-minute cycle looks like. It now also requires the FINISHED counter to be unchanged and at least forty-five minutes between readings. The self-test carries that case: seven of its twenty-one shapes are ones a moving system must NOT fire.
- Every fifteen minutes instead of thirty. A lane standing empty for half an hour is half an hour of a swarm that was asked never to stand still.
- What this does NOT establish: that `dispatchable` is exact. The tick reports how many candidates it skipped as claimed or completed but caps the lists it prints, so the count is a subtraction of counts and is named as an estimate in the rule that uses it.
