# NOW -- t27c test-report stops a test that outruns its limit and counts it as failed (2026-10-07)

## bootstrap/src/test_report.rs, bootstrap/tests/test_report_timeout.rs (Refs #7255)

- The defect: `test-report` runs each test as its own process and waited on it with no limit. A test that never returns hung `test-report`, and `seal --save` with it, for good; a kill from outside left the test binary spinning with PPID 1. Four tracked specs do this today (`specs/fpga/testbench/apb_bridge_tb`, `axi4_tb`, `clock_domain_tb`, `gf16_accel_tb`), and a fifth does not end either: `specs/ml/layers/residual_connection`'s `forward_basic_case` walks a slice built from `undefined`, as `default_input()` is declared nowhere (#5166).
- The fix: each test, and the listing that names them, runs under a limit: 60 s, Bazel's limit for a `small` test, or `T27C_TEST_TIMEOUT` seconds when that is a whole number above 0. A test that outruns it is killed and reaped, counted as failed, and its line says so: `- spins_forever   (timed out after 60 s, not counted)`. The other tests of the spec still run and are reported. The work dir is removed as before.
- Measured on the Railway lab, the 403b27f29 tree, `test-report --verbose` on each of the 1484 tracked `.t27` files, master's release t27c (under an outer limit of 150 s) against this change's (900 s), 4 at a time: master hit the outer limit on the four testbenches; this change finished them with exit 0 in 240.6, 240.7, 300.4 and 420.4 s, with 4, 4, 5 and 7 tests timed out and 2 of 8, 0 of 5, 3 of 8 and 3 of 10 passing. The other 1480 ended under both binaries; their output is byte-identical on 1479 once master's `/tmp/t27c-test-report-<stem>-<pid>/` prefix is cut (#7243 changes it). The one that differs is `residual_connection`: master 70.9 s and `(failed, not counted)`, this change 60.4 s and `(timed out after 60 s, not counted)`. Without it the 1479 took 613.8 s under master and 615.4 s here.
- The lab hid that fifth hang: another session runs `/data/ref/reaper.sh`, which kills any `t27c-test-report-*/spec_tests` older than 60 s every 20 s. Under master a hanging test there ends at 60 to 80 s as "failed"; on any other host it does not end. Each of the four master runs the outer limit killed left its work dir, and 2 processes (t27c and its test) to kill and reap; this change's runs left neither.
- Tests: in `test_report`, `the_timeout_is_a_whole_number_of_seconds_above_zero`, `a_timed_out_test_says_so_in_its_line` and `a_looping_test_is_stopped_and_counted_as_failed` (a probe whose first test spins and whose second returns, run with a 1 s limit: totals 2/1/1, `timed_out` 1 s, under 30 s, no work dir left); `tests/test_report_timeout.rs` runs the CLI with `T27C_TEST_TIMEOUT=2` and reads the lines. Both looping tests kill the probe themselves after 50 s and fail, so a regression fails the suite instead of hanging it.
- Negative controls on the lab: the environment variable not read turns `the_timeout_is_a_whole_number_of_seconds_above_zero` and the CLI test red (the CLI test waited the default 60 s and printed no "after 2 s"); the limit check removed, as on master, turns both looping tests red, each at its own outer bound and before the lab's reaper: the unit test after 50.03 s ("run_limited still ran after 50 s"), the CLI test after 50.01 s ("test-report still ran after 50s"), with no work dir left. The CLI test kills with `kill -9 -- -<pgid>`: procps-ng 4.0.2 reads `kill -9 -<pgid>` as no target, exits 0 and kills nothing.
- Out of scope, named in #7255: a `t27c` killed from outside still leaves its running test behind, and work dirs from killed runs stay in the temp dir.
- Foreign Rust under the standing owner rule (label owner-approved-foreign): `test_report.rs` is already listed in `tools/policy/foreign-exceptions.txt`; the new test is added there. `bootstrap/src/compiler.rs` and FROZEN_HASH are not touched.
