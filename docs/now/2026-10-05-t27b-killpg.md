# NOW -- t27b timeout kill no longer takes down the corpus driver (2026-10-05)

## Group kill through kill(2) (Closes #6361)

- Lab runs at d8375ad52 and 84e478b3f lost the corpus step with exit -9. `run_capture` stopped a timed-out child by spawning `kill -KILL -<pgid>`. The procps-ng 4.0.2 `kill` in the lab image reads that negative pid as -1, so the call signalled every process except PID 1.
- Measured on the lab: `/bin/kill -CONT -999999` exits 0 for a group that does not exist. With a no-op `kill` on PATH, the driver survived its timeouts.
- `cli/t27b/src/blockers.rs` now signals the group with `kill(2)` directly and refuses pgid 0 and 1. New test: `a_timeout_kills_the_group_and_spares_the_caller` in `cli/t27b/tests/blockers.rs`.
