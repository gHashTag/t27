# t27 analog emulation v0.1 -- release notes, 2026-10-24

Tag: `analog-v0.1.0`, on the merge commit of the pull request that adds this file. Epic: #10486 (milestone M6). English, ASCII only.

Every number below has its source next to it (a pull request, a test or a receipt in this repository); a number without a source is not here (success criterion SC-005 of the epic). Nothing below is a speed claim, a power claim or a claim about a chip.

## What v0.1 is

An analog circuit given as a SPICE netlist with IHP SG13G2 (PSP103) device models becomes `.t27` specs that are at once the software reference and the FPGA design. The specs live in `specs/analog/`, the netlist front end in `specs/tools/t27o/`, the board tops in `specs/fpga/` and `specs/analog/board/`. The checks run with `t27c test-report <spec>` and against ngspice-47 with the OSDI models, and a second reference, VACASK, agrees with ngspice on the blocks it ran.

The reference circuit is the SAR ADC of the golden deck (16 subcircuits, 1265 MOS after flattening). v0.1 covers its blocks and the first converter slice, one corner (tt) on the AX7203 board, and an acceptance evaluator that applies an external team's per-block checks to our models. It is not a full mixed-signal chip.

Corner and PDK: tt unless a row names another; the IHP-Open-PDK revision is named in each deck (21133f21 for the acceptance decks); 27 C unless a row says 50 C.

### Speed, stated plainly

The board is slower than real time and v0.1 makes no speed claim. One 100 ps backward Euler step of the 500 fF inverter takes 1700 to 1960 clocks at 100 MHz, which is 17.0 to 19.6 us, a real-time ratio of 170000 to 196000 times slower (`specs/analog/bench.t27`, tests `board_min` and `board_max`). ngspice-47's transient analysis of the same circuit took 19.56 us a time point (195623 times real time on the 4 us run) on one thread of a general-purpose CPU. The two are not the cost of the same work (ngspice evaluates the full PSP103 model in every Newton iteration; the board reads tables of it in exact-integer arithmetic), and the file draws no conclusion from them (#11313). "103.61 MHz" is the logic clock after routing, not the circuit's speed.

## What it does not claim

- No speed claim, no power claim, no noise, mismatch or temperature claim (the 50 C runs and the 27 C ones are named where they are).
- The two behavioural board tops (M1b, the SAR converter) do not read the PSP103 tables. The only top that does is the tt inverter. The comparator's phase 2a is a routed unit that FAILS timing at 100 MHz and was never loaded on the board; the comparator step on the board (phase 2b, #11380) is not built.
- One corner on the board at the tag. The other four corners had tables and ngspice checks but no bitstream then; their Build B receipts of 2026-10-24 are in `docs/analog/board-psp103-inv-buildb-2026-10-24.md`, each from a single run with nextpnr's post-route Fmax of 101.70 to 116.73 MHz and no speed claim.
- The converter is not predicted end to end. `sar_adc.t27` still takes fitted numbers (the top-plate load of 77 fF and an offset); S5 of #10942 is met against ngspice's in-situ balance but 3 of 4 against the issue's pooled thresholds. The comparator and the switch at PSP103 level are not yet inside the converter loop.
- The acceptance result is 9 of 13 blocks. dff (its levels row is produced and outside the bar), DAC_control, DAC_control_top and neg_bottom_plate_logic are NOT MODELLED. The integration run passes as a behavioural converter fitted to the golden deck. The report's corners smoke suite (5 corners, -40 to 125 C) is NOT MODELLED.
- The external team's own evaluator and its testbenches were not run by us; `accept_sar.t27` is our implementation of the published checks and limits.
- The full demo of the external team's VACASK example does not run on the VACASK obtained (it stops in hbac1 on the wheel 0.3.4.dev3); only the comparator and the DAC were compared.
- The body effect on the charge is not modelled; the comparator's resolution time is 4.9 % early and its nodes differ from ngspice's by up to 83 mV during regeneration (#11325).
- This is a SAR ADC and its blocks, not a full mixed-signal chip, not Verilog-A input and not PCIe.

## What comes after v0.1

One bitstream per remaining corner (ss, ff, fs, sf); the comparator step on the board with several evaluator units (#11380, its t27b rung 1 is #11376); the four NOT MODELLED blocks; S5 of #10942; the external team's second circuit when its block and criterion are named; a block of a public test chip (#11388, needs the authors' word); the Kintex-7 board over PCIe (M7).

## Reproduce

`t27c test-report specs/analog/<spec>.t27` for a spec's tests (vacuous passes must be 0). The decks under `specs/analog/decks/` run with ngspice-47 and the IHP-Open-PDK at the revision named in each deck. The board receipts and the exact build lines are in `docs/analog/board-regression-2026-10-19.md`, `docs/analog/board-psp103-inv-2026-10-20.md` and `docs/analog/board-sar-cmp-unit-2026-10-20.md`; the acceptance table with each row's function is `docs/analog/accept-sar-2026-10-20.md`; the sources of the first night's numbers are in `docs/analog/night-report-2026-10-19.md`. Runs that need ngspice and the PDK belong on the Railway lab, not on a laptop.
