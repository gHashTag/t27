A ternary node has three sites that could carry a number format; one needs to. The weight is a code, the sample arrives ADC-native, and only the accumulator has range to spend. The multiplier can then be removed rather than cheapened, and the scale permitting that is unique: with a two-bit {-phi,0,+phi} alphabet, Z[phi] is closed under weight application and accumulation, a weight is the Fibonacci step (a,b)->(b,a+b), and a layer's linear path is exact -- machine-checked in Coq with no admitted lemmas. A scale is multiplier-free exactly when it is an algebraic integer whose companion matrix has entries in {0,+-1}; degree two admits phi alone, and r^d=r+1 reaches any granularity at one addition and d registers. On an XC7A200T a ternary neuron costs 28 LUT per weight at fan-in 8, with no DSP at any fan-in.

Three negative results are stated plainly. First, the format names rungs in storage symbols: "TNF16" is a sign, four exponent trits and eleven mantissa bits -- nineteen bits. Rungs are renamed by bit width. Second, at every matched physical width posit holds more representable values and a finer step at unity (0.024% against 0.002% at 19 bits), because a ternary exponent leaves 8190 of 2^19 codes unreachable; posit with es=2 weakly dominates on both range and precision. Third, no frequency claim survives: fifteen of twenty-eight on-die clauses were constants folded at synthesis, so every published area measured a design with its checks deleted, and re-measurement gives Fmax = 3174*LUT^-0.648 (R^2=0.92), making MHz per LUT a proxy for size. The area result and zero-DSP stand.

Twenty-two retractions of our own claims are marked in place.
