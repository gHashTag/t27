  A full page is a LOWER BOUND and only a short one is a total, so a
  bounded fetch that prints what it got is stating a page as a census. The
  subject of the guard question is the ENCLOSING FUNCTION, which has a cost
  this states rather than hides: a function that only BUILDS a url and hands
  it to a guarded caller reads as unguarded, and `red.rs`'s `runs_url` is
  exactly that. 2 of the 2 in that bucket are that shape.

  The exclusions are the other half of the reading. Taking every line that
  merely NAMES `--limit` or `per_page=` reports 65 sites in a crate that has
  25: prose, doc comments, a marker table and test assertions. A matcher that
  swallowed them would be describing its input.

  SURFACE: this reads `cli/tri/src/*.rs` and nothing else. 4 bounded
  read(s) of the same API live in `scripts/tri_loop/*.py`; 4 sit in a file
  that says a filled page is a LOWER BOUND and 0 do not. Counted by FILE,
  not per read, and by the Rust side's own wording so the two surfaces
  cannot drift into two vocabularies. Named here rather than left silent,
  because a census that excludes part of its subject without saying so is
  this command's own subject.
