A ternary node has three sites that could carry a number format; one needs to. The weight is a code, the sample arrives ADC-native, only the accumulator has range to spend. The multiplier is then removed rather than cheapened: with a two-bit {-phi,0,+phi} alphabet, Z[phi] is closed under weight application and accumulation, a weight is the Fibonacci step (a,b)->(b,a+b), and a layer's linear path is exact -- machine-checked in Coq, no admitted lemmas. A scale is multiplier-free at one addition and no shifter whenever its companion matrix has entries in {0,+-1}; sufficient, not necessary, since {0,+-2^k} are shifts. Degree two then admits phi alone. On an XC7A200T a ternary neuron costs 28 LUT per weight at fan-in 8, no DSP at any fan-in.
